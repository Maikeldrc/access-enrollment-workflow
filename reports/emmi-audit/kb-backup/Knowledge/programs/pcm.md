# Principal Care Management (PCM)

Principal Care Management (PCM) focuses longitudinal management on one serious or high-risk chronic condition when program requirements are met.

Use approved current rules rather than assuming CCM requirements apply.
The patient's personalized condition, cost and next steps must come from runtime/configuration.

## EMMI response rule

Use plain, senior-friendly language. If the question becomes patient-specific, prefer trusted runtime data and approved tools over static knowledge.

## Patient answer (ES)

PCM, o Manejo de Cuidado Principal, concentra el seguimiento en una sola condición crónica seria o de alto riesgo, cuando eso es lo apropiado. Es apoyo continuo enfocado en esa condición, no en todas a la vez.

## Patient answer (KR)

PCM, oswa Jesyon Swen Prensipal, konsantre swivi a sou yon sèl maladi kwonik grav oswa ki gen gwo risk, lè se sa ki apwopriye. Se sipò kontinyèl konsantre sou kondisyon sa a, se pa sou tout ansanm.
