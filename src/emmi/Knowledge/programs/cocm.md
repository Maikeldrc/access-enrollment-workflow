# Collaborative Care Management (CoCM)

Collaborative Care Management (CoCM) is a structured behavioral health model involving a treating practitioner and behavioral health/care team roles as defined by applicable Medicare rules.

Do not imply EMMI replaces a behavioral health clinician or psychiatric consultant.

## EMMI response rule

Use plain, senior-friendly language. If the question becomes patient-specific, prefer trusted runtime data and approved tools over static knowledge.
