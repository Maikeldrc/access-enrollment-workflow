# ACCESS tracks: eCKM, CKM, MSK and BH

**eCKM stands for Early Cardio-Kidney-Metabolic.** It is one of the four tracks CMS launched for the ACCESS model, and it is the answer to "what does eCKM mean?".

CMS launched four tracks:

- **eCKM — Early Cardio-Kidney-Metabolic.** Hypertension, or certain combinations of high cholesterol or lipids, overweight or obesity with a central obesity marker, and prediabetes.
- **CKM — Cardio-Kidney-Metabolic.** Diabetes, chronic kidney disease stage 3a or 3b, or atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease.
- **MSK — Musculoskeletal.** Chronic musculoskeletal pain.
- **BH — Behavioural Health.** Depression and anxiety.

A participating organisation may offer only some of these tracks. Exact eligibility for any of them is confirmed by CMS and ITERA, not by a description on this page.

## Saying it in plain words

A patient asking what eCKM means is usually asking why they are in it. Give them the words the letters stand for and the plain-language version: it is the ACCESS track for people managing early heart, kidney and metabolic conditions — most often high blood pressure — before they become more serious.

Do not spell the acronym out and stop there. "Early Cardio-Kidney-Metabolic" is not an explanation on its own.

## More than one track

A patient can qualify for more than one track, and CMS permits participation in different tracks. Which tracks apply, and which organisations are available for them, has to be checked rather than assumed.

## EMMI response rule

Answer what a track is from this page. Which track *this* patient is on, and whether they qualify for another, are facts about them: those come from the runtime enrollment context, never from here.

Never quote a monthly amount from this page — it carries none. Any amount comes from the financial responsibility engine for that patient and that track.
