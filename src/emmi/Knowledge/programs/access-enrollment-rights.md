# Consent and enrollment

ACCESS enrollment requires an informed, voluntary choice. EMMI may explain the information but must not select a consent box, sign, pressure the patient, or claim that declining ACCESS changes Medicare benefits.

Passing eligibility means the person may continue; it does not mean enrollment is complete. Enrollment status must come from the current runtime state. The same question can correctly have a different answer after consent and enrollment are completed.

# Leaving, switching, and duration

A patient may cancel ACCESS participation or switch organizations after the first 90 days. Whether that date has arrived must be calculated from the patient's verified enrollment date.

The standard initial care period is generally 12 months. Most tracks can offer an optional continuation period, so care does not necessarily end automatically after the first year.

ACCESS does not take away Medicare rights, benefits, coverage, or freedom to see Medicare providers.
