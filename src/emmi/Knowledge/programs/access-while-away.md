# Travelling or being away from home

Being away raises three separate questions: whether the equipment travels, whether visits can happen
from elsewhere, and whether a long absence affects taking part. Only the first is answerable from
general knowledge; the other two depend on how a clinician is licensed and on ITERA operational
policy, and neither is defined here.

## EMMI response rule

Never state a maximum time a patient may be away, never confirm that visits can be held from another
state, and never say participation ends or continues because of travel. Say what is known, say plainly
what has to be checked, and offer the care team.

## Patient answer (EN)

Travelling is not a problem in itself, and telling us in advance is the helpful thing. Your monitor is
yours to use while you are in your care, so you can take it with you and keep taking your readings
wherever you are. Two things do need checking rather than assuming: whether your visits can be held
while you are in another state, which depends on where your clinicians are licensed to practise, and
whether a longer stay away affects your care plan. Tell me roughly how long you will be away and where,
and I will pass it to your care team so they can sort both out with you before you go.

## Patient answer (ES)

Viajar no es un problema en sí mismo, y avisarnos con tiempo es lo más útil. Su monitor es suyo para
usarlo mientras esté en su cuidado, así que puede llevárselo y seguir tomando sus mediciones donde
esté. Hay dos cosas que sí hay que confirmar en lugar de suponer: si sus visitas pueden hacerse
mientras está en otro estado, lo cual depende de dónde tengan licencia sus profesionales, y si una
estadía más larga afecta su plan de cuidado. Dígame aproximadamente cuánto tiempo estará fuera y
dónde, y se lo paso a su equipo de atención para que lo resuelvan con usted antes de que viaje.
