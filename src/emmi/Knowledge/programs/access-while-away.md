# Travelling or being away from home

Being away raises three separate questions: whether the equipment travels, whether visits can happen
from elsewhere, and whether a long absence affects taking part. Only the first is answerable from
general knowledge; the other two depend on how a clinician is licensed and on ITERA operational
policy, and neither is defined here.

## EMMI response rule

Never state a maximum time a patient may be away, and never say participation ends or continues
because of travel.

ITERA's clinicians are licensed in **Florida only**. That may be stated, and it answers the question
patients actually ask. Keep the two facts apart, because patients conflate them: the monitor works
anywhere in the United States and the readings keep arriving, while a visit by video depends on
licensure. Never name a state that is not on this list — a wrong state here sends someone to a visit
that cannot legally happen.

## Patient answer (EN)

Travelling is not a problem in itself, and telling us in advance is the helpful thing. Your monitor is
yours to use while you are in your care, so you can take it with you and keep taking your readings
wherever you are. Your readings will keep reaching your care team anywhere in the
United States. Visits by video are the part that depends on where you are: your care team is licensed
in Florida, so while you are in another state a video visit is not something they can hold. What a
longer stay means for your care plan is worth checking too. Tell me roughly how long you will be away
and where, and I will pass it to your care team so they can sort it out with you before you go.

## Patient answer (ES)

Viajar no es un problema en sí mismo, y avisarnos con tiempo es lo más útil. Su monitor es suyo para
usarlo mientras esté en su cuidado, así que puede llevárselo y seguir tomando sus mediciones donde
esté. Sus mediciones seguirán llegando a su equipo desde cualquier lugar de
Estados Unidos. Lo que sí depende de dónde esté son las visitas por vídeo: su equipo tiene licencia en
Florida, así que mientras esté en otro estado no pueden atenderle por vídeo. También conviene revisar
qué supone una estancia larga para su plan de cuidado. Dígame aproximadamente cuánto tiempo estará
fuera y dónde, y se lo paso a su equipo para que lo resuelvan con usted antes de que viaje.
