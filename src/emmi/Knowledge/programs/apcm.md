# Advanced Primary Care Management (APCM)

Advanced Primary Care Management (APCM) supports ongoing primary care management under applicable Medicare rules.

Do not invent program requirements, cost, practitioner relationships or billing rules.
Use approved current CMS/ITERA configuration for patient-facing answers.

## EMMI response rule

Use plain, senior-friendly language. If the question becomes patient-specific, prefer trusted runtime data and approved tools over static knowledge.

## Patient answer (ES)

APCM, o Manejo Avanzado de Atención Primaria, es apoyo continuo para su atención primaria bajo las reglas de Medicare que apliquen. Su médico de cabecera sigue siendo su médico.

## Patient answer (KR)

APCM, oswa Jesyon Swen Prensipal Avanse, se sipò kontinyèl pou swen prensipal ou dapre règ Medicare ki aplike yo. Doktè prensipal ou rete doktè ou.
