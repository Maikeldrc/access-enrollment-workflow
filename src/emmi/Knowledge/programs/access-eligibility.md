# Who can take part in ACCESS

Medicare states that ACCESS is open to anyone with Original Medicare who has one or more of the
qualifying chronic conditions, and that people in a Medicare Advantage plan cannot use it. A referral
is not required: a person can contact an approved organization directly, and may also ask a
pharmacist, doctor or other provider for help doing so. Someone with more than one qualifying
condition may work with more than one approved organization.

Qualifying conditions named by Medicare are high blood pressure, high cholesterol (dyslipidemia),
obesity, prediabetes, diabetes, chronic kidney disease, heart disease (atherosclerotic cardiovascular
disease), depression, anxiety, and ongoing muscle or joint pain. Medicare notes that more conditions
may be added.

Medicare lists three reasons a person may not be able to use ACCESS: not meeting the eligibility
requirements for the condition they want support for, being in a Medicare Advantage plan, and being
randomly assigned to the evaluation comparison group.

## EMMI response rule

Whether a particular patient is eligible is a fact about them and comes from the runtime enrollment
context, never from this page. This page answers what the requirements are. Living in a nursing home
or an assisted living facility, and being in hospice, are not listed by Medicare either way: they
must be routed to the care team rather than answered from here.

Medicare states the requirement as having Original Medicare. It does not publish a rule for a
patient who holds only Part A, or only Part B, so neither case has an answer here. A patient who
says which parts they hold must not be told yes or no: say that which parts they have is part of
their verified coverage, and offer to have it checked. Answering "yes, you can still take part with
only Part A" is an invention, and it is the kind a patient acts on.

## Patient answer (EN)

ACCESS is open to people with Original Medicare who have one or more of the conditions it covers.
Those are high blood pressure, high cholesterol, obesity, prediabetes, diabetes, chronic kidney
disease, heart disease, depression, anxiety, and ongoing muscle or joint pain, and Medicare may add
more over time. You do not need a referral, and if you have more than one of these conditions you may
be able to work with more than one approved organization. If you are in a Medicare Advantage plan you
cannot use ACCESS, though your plan may offer something similar. Whether you personally qualify is
part of your own record, and I can check that with you. If you are not sure which parts of Medicare
you have, that is worth confirming rather than guessing, and your care team can look it up with you.

## Patient answer (ES)

ACCESS es para personas con Medicare Original que tienen una o más de las condiciones que cubre:
presión alta, colesterol alto, obesidad, prediabetes, diabetes, enfermedad renal crónica, enfermedad
del corazón, depresión, ansiedad y dolor muscular o de articulaciones de larga duración. Medicare
puede añadir más con el tiempo. No necesita un referido, y si tiene más de una de estas condiciones
puede trabajar con más de una organización aprobada. Si tiene un plan Medicare Advantage no puede
usar ACCESS, aunque su plan puede ofrecer algo parecido. Si usted califica es parte de su expediente,
y puedo revisarlo con usted.
