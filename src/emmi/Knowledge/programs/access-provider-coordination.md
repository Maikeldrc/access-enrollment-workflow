# Keeping existing clinicians

ACCESS complements traditional care. Patients keep their regular doctors and specialists and may contact those offices directly. The identity and role of a patient's doctor or care-team member must be confirmed from runtime data.

# Care updates

ACCESS organizations provide secure electronic care updates to primary care and referring clinicians at key points: care initiation, including the care plan and baseline measures; care escalation, when clinical needs change or care is transitioned; and care completion, with an end-of-period summary.

Do not promise that a named clinician sees every raw device reading or receives it immediately unless the runtime workflow confirms that. Explain the required update points and verify actual delivery or status through runtime when available.

ACCESS is not a 24/7 emergency monitoring service unless the implemented workflow explicitly says otherwise. Emergency concerns must follow the safety pathway.
