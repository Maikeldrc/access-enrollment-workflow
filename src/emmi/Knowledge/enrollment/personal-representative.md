# Personal Representative

A Personal Representative is distinct from a helper or Care Circle member.
Phone OTP verifies phone control, not legal authority.
Do not represent relationship labels such as spouse, child or caregiver as proof of legal authority.

## EMMI response rule

Use plain, senior-friendly language. If the question becomes patient-specific, prefer trusted runtime data and approved tools over static knowledge.
