# Leaving ACCESS, ending participation or changing provider: the 90 day term

Participation in ACCESS is voluntary: the patient chooses whether to enrol.

**Beginning 90 days after enrollment, the patient may end their ACCESS participation or switch to another participating provider.**

The ninety days is a term of the consent the patient agrees to, and it is displayed on the consent
screen before they agree.

Ending ACCESS participation does not end or change the patient's Medicare. Their normal Medicare
benefits, coverage and rights continue.

## What must never be said

Never tell a patient they can leave "whenever they want", "at any time", or "cuando quiera". That
omits the ninety days and misrepresents what they agreed to.

Never imply the patient is locked in permanently either. The ninety days is a delay before leaving
becomes possible, not a permanent commitment.

## Leaving is not losing Medicare

Ending ACCESS participation does not end or change the patient's Medicare. Their normal Medicare
benefits, coverage and rights continue, exactly as they do for someone who never enrolled.

## EMMI response rule

Answer from this page: participation is voluntary, and beginning 90 days after enrollment the
patient may leave ACCESS or switch to another participating provider. That sentence is the answer to
"when can I leave?", "how long am I committing to?" and "can I change provider?", and it must appear
in any answer to them.

Never tell a patient they may leave "whenever they want", "at any time", or "cuando quiera": that
omits the ninety days and misrepresents what they agreed to.

EMMI does not end anyone's participation and does not switch anyone's provider. Once the patient has
the timing, the action itself goes to the care team, and only after the patient explicitly asks for
it.

What a particular patient would pay, and whether they are enrolled at all, are facts about them and
come from the runtime tools rather than from this page.

## Patient answer (ES)

Participar en ACCESS es voluntario. A partir de 90 días después de la inscripción, usted puede terminar su participación en ACCESS o cambiar a otro proveedor participante. Dejar ACCESS no termina ni cambia su Medicare: sus beneficios, su cobertura y sus derechos continúan igual.
