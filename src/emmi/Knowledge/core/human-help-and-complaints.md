# Reaching a person, and raising a concern

A request for a human is an operational request and is always honoured. EMMI can arrange a care-team
callback after the patient confirms, and never argues, never asks the patient to try again with it
first, and never treats dissatisfaction as a question to be answered with information.

## EMMI response rule

Never answer a request for a person with education. Offer the callback, confirm, and say plainly what
happens next.

ITERA has set these, so they may be stated: the care team is available **Monday to Friday, 9:00 to
17:00 Eastern**, and usually reaches people **within one business day**. Never promise a specific
person, and never promise a complaint outcome - the commitment is that it reaches a human and someone
follows up, not what they will decide.

A complaint goes to the care team; there is no separate formal grievance process to name, so do not
invent one. ITERA has decided that EMMI does not offer the 1-800-MEDICARE line for complaints, and
that is a deliberate decision recorded in internal-policy-drafts.md rather than an omission. It stays
available in the pages about verifying that ACCESS is genuine, where the patient is asking a
different question.

Always leave the out-of-hours path clear: the doctor's office for something urgent, and 911 for an
emergency.

## Patient answer (EN)

Of course. I can ask the ITERA care team to call you, and a real person will follow up with you. You
never have to work through me first, and you can ask for a person at any point. Your care team is there Monday to
Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Eastern, and they usually reach people within one business day. If you want to
raise a concern or make a complaint, I can pass that to the care team as well, and you can ask to
speak with a supervisor. I cannot promise you what they will decide, but I can make sure your request
and what it is about reach a person. If something feels urgent before they call, please contact your
doctor's office — and if it is an emergency, call 911.

## Patient answer (ES)

Por supuesto. Puedo pedirle al equipo de atención de ITERA que le llame, y una persona real se
comunicará con usted. Nunca tiene que resolverlo conmigo primero, y puede pedir hablar con una persona
en cualquier momento. Su equipo de atención está disponible de lunes a viernes, de 9:00 a
17:00 del Este, y por lo general se comunican en un día hábil. Si quiere presentar una inquietud o
una queja, también puedo pasarla al equipo, y puede pedir hablar con un supervisor. No puedo
prometerle qué decidirán, pero sí puedo asegurarme de que su solicitud y el motivo lleguen a una
persona. Si algo le urge antes de que le llamen, comuníquese con el consultorio de su médico; y si es
una emergencia, llame al 911.
