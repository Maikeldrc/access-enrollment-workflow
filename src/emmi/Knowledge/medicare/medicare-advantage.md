# Medicare Advantage

Medicare Advantage plans are private plans that provide Medicare-covered benefits under plan rules.
Do not treat Medicare Advantage as interchangeable with Original Medicare for program eligibility.
ACCESS eligibility in this project is restricted to applicable Original Medicare / FFS scenarios.
