# Supplemental insurance

Supplemental insurance is coverage a person may hold in addition to Original Medicare. The most common form is Medicare Supplement Insurance, often called Medigap, which can help pay some of the costs Original Medicare leaves to the patient.

For how Medigap itself works, see the Medigap page. For how a primary and a secondary payer work together, see the coordination of benefits page.

## Having supplemental coverage does not by itself mean a cost is covered

This is the point that matters most, and the one most easily got wrong.

Three separate things have to be true before a supplement reduces what a patient pays for a specific service: the policy has to exist, it has to be active and verified now rather than at some earlier point, and it has to actually apply to the cost in question.

A patient can hold an active, verified supplement and still owe the full amount for a particular service, because that service is not something their policy pays.

## Never promise coverage from the existence of a policy

Do not tell a patient their supplement covers something because they have a supplement. Say only what verified coverage data says, and if that data does not establish whether the policy applies to this cost, say the amount is not yet known.

## EMMI response rule

Use this page for the general idea. Whether this patient has supplemental coverage, which carrier it is, and whether it covers a particular cost are patient-specific facts that come from verified coverage data and the financial responsibility engine.

## Patient answer (ES)

El seguro suplementario es una cobertura adicional a Medicare Original. La forma más común es el seguro suplementario de Medicare, llamado Medigap, que puede ayudar a pagar parte de lo que Medicare Original deja a su cargo. Tenerlo no significa por sí solo que un costo esté cubierto: lo que paga depende de la póliza y del servicio.

Asirans siplemantè se yon kouvèti anplis Medicare Orijinal. Fòm ki pi kouran an se asirans siplemantè Medicare, yo rele l Medigap, ki ka ede peye yon pati nan sa Medicare Orijinal kite sou do ou. Genyen l pou kont li pa vle di yon depans kouvri: sa li peye depann de polis la ak sèvis la.
