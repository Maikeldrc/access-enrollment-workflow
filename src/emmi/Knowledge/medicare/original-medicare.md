# Original Medicare

Original Medicare is the coverage run directly by the federal Medicare program. It is made up of two parts.

## Part A

Part A is hospital insurance. It covers inpatient hospital care and certain other services, under Medicare's rules for each type of care.

## Part B

Part B is medical insurance. It covers doctor visits, outpatient care and a range of other services, under Medicare's rules for each type of care.

## What Medicare pays and what may be left to the patient

For many covered Part B services, Medicare pays most of the Medicare-approved amount and the patient may be responsible for part of the remaining cost, depending on the service and their circumstances.

A commonly encountered arrangement for Part B services is that, after applicable deductible rules, Medicare pays 80% of the Medicare-approved amount and the beneficiary is generally responsible for the remaining 20% as coinsurance.

That 80/20 pattern is common but it is not a universal rule for every Medicare service. Never tell a patient Medicare always pays 80% of everything.

## Original Medicare is not Medicare Advantage

Having Part A and Part B active does not by itself establish that someone has Original Medicare, because Medicare Advantage members have Part A and Part B as well. Which one a patient has is a fact that comes from their coverage data, not an inference.
