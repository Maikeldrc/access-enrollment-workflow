# When your insurance changes

Coverage is not permanent, and a change can affect both what a patient is expected to pay and whether
they can take part at all. Moving from Original Medicare to a Medicare Advantage plan is the change
that ends ACCESS eligibility, because Medicare states that people in a Medicare Advantage plan cannot
use ACCESS.

## EMMI response rule

Never state an expected amount after a patient reports a coverage change: the previous verification no
longer stands. Never tell a patient they have lost, or kept, their place in ACCESS. Route the change
to the care team so coverage can be verified again.

## Patient answer (EN)

Thank you for telling me, and yes, please always let us know when your insurance changes. It matters
for two reasons: what you are expected to pay is worked out from your verified coverage, and ACCESS
is only for people with Original Medicare. Because your coverage has changed, the amount I had is no
longer current, so I do not want to quote you a figure that may be wrong. Let me get your care team to
re-check your coverage and tell you exactly where you stand, including what it means for taking part
if you have moved to a Medicare Advantage plan.

## Patient answer (ES)

Gracias por decírmelo, y sí, por favor avísenos siempre que cambie su seguro. Importa por dos razones:
lo que se espera que usted pague se calcula con su cobertura verificada, y ACCESS es solo para
personas con Medicare Original. Como su cobertura cambió, la cantidad que yo tenía ya no está
actualizada, y no quiero darle una cifra que pueda estar equivocada. Voy a pedir a su equipo de
atención que verifique su cobertura de nuevo y le diga exactamente cómo queda, incluyendo qué
significa para su participación si pasó a un plan Medicare Advantage.

## Patient answer (KR)

Mèsi paske ou di m sa, epi wi, tanpri toujou fè nou konnen lè asirans ou chanje. Sa enpòtan pou de
rezon: sa yo prevwa pou w peye kalkile ak kouvèti yo verifye a, epi ACCESS se sèlman pou moun ki gen
Medicare Orijinal. Paske kouvèti ou chanje, montan mwen te genyen an pa aktyèl ankò, epi mwen pa vle
ba w yon chif ki ka pa kòrèk. Kite m mande ekip swen ou verifye kouvèti ou ankò epi di w egzakteman
kote ou ye, ansanm ak sa sa vle di pou patisipasyon ou si ou pase nan yon plan Medicare Advantage.
