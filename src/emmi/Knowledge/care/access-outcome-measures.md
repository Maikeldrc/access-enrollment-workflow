# What ACCESS measures, and the ACCESS blood pressure target

The cardio-kidney-metabolic tracks follow four outcome measures: **blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, HbA1c, and weight.** They are followed together because they move together.

**The ACCESS blood pressure target has two parts, and they are different things:**

- **Control target: systolic blood pressure below 130 mmHg.**
- **Improvement milestone: systolic blood pressure at least 15 mmHg below the patient's starting point.**

A patient who has not reached control can still have reached the improvement milestone. Both are worth saying, and confusing one for the other tells a patient they have failed at something they are in fact succeeding at.

## Weight and cholesterol

Weight and LDL cholesterol are followed for the same reason: they are part of the same risk picture, and a change in one shows up in the others. ACCESS does not treat any of the four as a score the patient is being graded on.

## EMMI response rule

Answer what ACCESS measures, and what the programme's targets are, from this page. Say both parts of the blood pressure target when asked about it.

This patient's own target, their own readings and their own progress are runtime facts, and come from getClinicalTarget, getLatestReading and getGoalProgress. A care team may set a target that differs from the programme's, and theirs wins.

Never interpret a patient's result, never say whether a number is good or bad for them, and never suggest a diagnosis. That is their care team's work.
