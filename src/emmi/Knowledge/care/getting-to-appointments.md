# Getting to your appointments

Not being able to get to care is one of the most common reasons care does not happen, and it is
something the care team works on with the patient. It is recorded as a barrier so it is visible and
followed up, rather than answered as a topic.

## EMMI response rule

This is an action, not an explanation. Record the difficulty and offer to bring in the care team.
EMMI cannot book, order or pay for a ride, and must never say or imply that transportation is covered,
free, arranged, or paid for by ITERA, Medicare or ACCESS: whether any transportation help exists, who
provides it and who pays for it is an ITERA operational policy that is not defined here. Say what can
be done — record it and get a person onto it — and never promise a ride.

## Patient answer (EN)

Thank you for telling me, and I am sorry it is difficult. Getting there matters, so let me record
this for your care team and ask them to work on it with you. I want to be straight with you about what
I can and cannot do: I cannot book a ride, order a taxi or a rideshare, or arrange transportation
myself, and I do not want to promise that a ride will be provided or paid for, because that is not
something I can confirm. What I can do is make sure your care team knows, so they can go through what
is available to you, including moving the appointment to a day that works better or seeing whether the
visit can be done from home. If you use a wheelchair or a walker, or you need help getting into a
vehicle, tell me and I will include that so nobody has to ask you twice.

## Patient answer (ES)

Gracias por decírmelo, y lamento que sea difícil. Llegar a la cita importa, así que voy a registrarlo
para su equipo de atención y pedirles que lo trabajen con usted. Quiero ser claro sobre lo que puedo y
no puedo hacer: no puedo reservar un viaje, pedir un taxi o un servicio de transporte, ni organizar el
traslado yo mismo, y no quiero prometerle que le darán o le pagarán un viaje, porque eso no lo puedo
confirmar. Lo que sí puedo hacer es asegurarme de que su equipo lo sepa, para que revisen con usted
qué opciones existen, incluyendo cambiar la cita a un día que le convenga más o ver si la visita puede
hacerse desde casa. Si usa silla de ruedas o andador, o necesita ayuda para subir al vehículo,
dígamelo y lo incluyo para que no tenga que explicarlo dos veces.

## Patient answer (KR)

Mèsi paske ou di m sa, epi mwen regrèt ke se difisil. Rive nan randevou a enpòtan, kidonk kite m
anrejistre sa pou ekip swen ou epi mande yo travay sou li avè w. Mwen vle di w klè sa mwen ka fè ak sa
mwen pa ka fè: mwen pa ka rezève yon woulib, rele yon taksi, ni ranje transpò mwen menm, epi mwen pa
vle pwomèt ke yo pral ba w oswa peye yon woulib, paske mwen pa ka konfime sa. Sa mwen ka fè se asire m
ekip swen ou konnen, pou yo ka gade avè w ki opsyon ki genyen, tankou chanje randevou a pou yon jou ki
pi bon oswa wè si vizit la ka fèt lakay ou. Si ou sèvi ak yon chèz woulant oswa yon machwè, oswa ou
bezwen èd pou antre nan yon machin, di m epi m ap mete sa ladan.
