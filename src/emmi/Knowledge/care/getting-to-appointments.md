# Getting to your appointments

Not being able to get to care is one of the most common reasons care does not happen, and it is
something the care team works on with the patient. It is recorded as a barrier so it is visible and
followed up, rather than answered as a topic.

## EMMI response rule

This is an action, not an explanation. Record the difficulty and offer to bring in the care team.
EMMI cannot book, order or pay for a ride, and must never say or imply that transportation is
covered, free, arranged, or paid for by ITERA, Medicare or ACCESS. ITERA has decided this: **ACCESS
does not include a transportation benefit.** Say so plainly rather than hedging - a patient who is
told "I cannot confirm" keeps waiting for an answer, and a patient who is told there is no benefit
can start arranging something else today. Then do what can be done: record it, get a person onto it,
and point them at their plan and local services. Never promise a ride.

## Patient answer (EN)

Thank you for telling me, and I am sorry it is difficult. Getting there matters, so let me record
this for your care team and ask them to work on it with you. I want to be straight with you: transportation is not
part of what ACCESS covers, so I cannot book a ride, order a taxi, or arrange one to be paid for.
Telling you that now is better than leaving you waiting for a ride that is not coming. What I can do
is make sure your care team knows, so they can go through what is available to you — your Medicare
plan or local services near you may have options — and look at moving the appointment to a day that
works better, or whether the visit can be done from home. If you use a wheelchair or a walker, or you need help getting into a
vehicle, tell me and I will include that so nobody has to ask you twice.

## Patient answer (ES)

Gracias por decírmelo, y lamento que sea difícil. Llegar a la cita importa, así que voy a registrarlo
para su equipo de atención y pedirles que lo trabajen con usted. Quiero serle claro: el transporte no
forma parte de lo que cubre ACCESS, así que no puedo reservarle un viaje, pedir un taxi ni gestionar
que se lo paguen. Prefiero decírselo ahora antes que dejarle esperando un traslado que no va a
llegar. Lo que sí puedo hacer es asegurarme de que su equipo lo sepa, para que revisen con usted qué
opciones existen — su plan de Medicare o los servicios locales de su zona pueden tener alguna — y
valorar cambiar la cita a un día que le convenga más, o si la visita puede hacerse desde casa. Si usa silla de ruedas o andador, o necesita ayuda para subir al vehículo,
dígamelo y lo incluyo para que no tenga que explicarlo dos veces.

## Patient answer (KR)

Mèsi paske ou di m sa, epi mwen regrèt ke se difisil. Rive nan randevou a enpòtan, kidonk kite m
anrejistre sa pou ekip swen ou epi mande yo travay sou li avè w. Mwen vle di w klè: transpò pa fè pati sa
ACCESS kouvri, kidonk mwen pa ka rezève yon woulib, rele yon taksi, ni fè yo peye youn. Mwen pito di
w sa kounye a pase kite w ap tann yon woulib ki p ap vini. Sa mwen ka fè se asire m ekip swen ou
konnen, pou yo ka gade avè w ki opsyon ki genyen — plan Medicare ou oswa sèvis lokal toupre w ka gen
kèk — epi wè si nou ka chanje randevou a pou yon jou ki pi bon, oswa si vizit la ka fèt lakay ou. Si ou sèvi ak yon chèz woulant oswa yon machwè, oswa ou
bezwen èd pou antre nan yon machin, di m epi m ap mete sa ladan.
