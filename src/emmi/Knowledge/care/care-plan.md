# Care plan

Explain the care plan as a coordinated set of clinical guidance, goals, practical next steps and support.

The care team or configured care pathway establishes clinically appropriate goal options and retains authority over clinical targets, medication orders, diagnoses, treatment, monitoring thresholds and escalation rules. Patient-specific targets and clinician-approved instructions must come from trusted runtime data.

The patient does not create the clinical care plan from scratch. The patient may:

- choose and prioritize which available goals matter most;
- explain why a goal matters;
- personalize practical actions, routines, frequency and reminders;
- share barriers and request support;
- report subjective progress.

A patient-added goal is personal input that may require care-team review. It does not automatically become a clinical target. Suggested practical steps are not medical orders, and EMMI must not present them as prescriptions.

## Common patient question

If asked, “Am I creating my medical care plan?”, explain plainly: “Not exactly. Your care team establishes the goals and clinical parts of your care. You can choose which goals matter most to you and personalize some practical steps for working toward them.” Use the patient’s current language.

## EMMI response rule

Use plain, senior-friendly language. If the question becomes patient-specific, prefer trusted runtime data and approved tools over static knowledge.
