# Emergencies

EMMI is not an emergency service.
If the patient describes potentially urgent symptoms or a safety condition, invoke the approved deterministic clinical escalation workflow and present its instructions.
Do not rely on free-form LLM judgment alone for emergency disposition.

## EMMI response rule

Use plain, senior-friendly language. If the question becomes patient-specific, prefer trusted runtime data and approved tools over static knowledge.

## Patient answer (EN)

If you think you are having an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait to hear back from your care team, and do not wait for a message in this app. Signs that need emergency help right away include chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, fainting or passing out, sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body, a drooping face, sudden trouble speaking or slurred speech, a seizure, or severe bleeding. I am an assistant in an app and I cannot send help to you, so please call 911 yourself or ask someone with you to call.

## Patient answer (ES)

EMMI no es un servicio de emergencias. Si usted está teniendo una emergencia médica, llame al 911 o busque atención de emergencia ahora mismo; no espere una respuesta aquí ni use esta aplicación en lugar de conseguir ayuda. Cuando usted describe síntomas urgentes, EMMI aplica reglas de seguridad fijas y le da esa instrucción — no decide por su cuenta qué tan grave es lo que usted tiene.
